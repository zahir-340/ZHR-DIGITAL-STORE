⚡ How Does VPN Work?

A VPN creates an encrypted connection between your device and a VPN server. Your internet traffic is routed through that server, and websites generally see the server's IP address instead of your original public IP address.

VPN Benefits

• Helps protect traffic on supported connections

• Uses a VPN server IP address

• Can route supported internet traffic through a server
